Inside the audio engine

Protection that responds to the sound itself.

A real-time Web Audio processor analyzes short windows of each protected tab, identifies risky patterns, and applies only the reduction that the selected comfort profile calls for.

01

Frequency-based softening

A nine-band comfort EQ lets listeners reduce sensitive ranges from 63 Hz through 12 kHz without turning everything down.

02

Beep blocking

A fast Fourier transform finds prominent narrow tones. Up to six short-lived notch filters follow confirmed beeps and release when they disappear.

03

Alarm reduction

Repeated multi-tone alarm signatures can trigger wider, faster notches and controlled signal ducking for more aggressive relief.

04

Speech preservation

The default speech-safe path raises detection requirements and demands stable high-frequency evidence before reducing the complete signal.

05

Sudden-peak limiting

A separate envelope limiter catches abrupt broadband peaks against a configurable ceiling, with quick attack and smooth recovery.

06

Look-ahead response

A 40 ms look-ahead buffer gives the processor time to analyze what is coming and soften it before it reaches the output.

The experience

Quick protection. Detailed control.

Start with a simple tab toggle, then tune the behavior only if your comfort needs call for it.

Private by architecture

Your audio stays on your device.

Processing happens locally in the browser. Sonic Shielding does not record audio, upload it, or send it to a remote analysis service. It also releases processing resources while protected audio is silent.
