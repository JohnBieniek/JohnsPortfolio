Audio processing

Sonic Shielding processes audio from browser tabs that you explicitly protect. Processing occurs locally and in real time on your device. Sonic Shielding does not record, retain, transmit, sell, or share audio.

Locally stored information

Your comfort-profile settings and the list of currently protected tabs are stored locally through Chrome extension storage. This information is used only to provide the extension’s features.

Information not collected

Sonic Shielding does not collect browsing history, analytics, personal information, or health information, and does not use a remote server.

Removing your data

Removing the extension removes its local extension data, subject to your browser’s storage behavior.

Questions

Questions may be submitted through the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing or the Sonic Shielding support page.
