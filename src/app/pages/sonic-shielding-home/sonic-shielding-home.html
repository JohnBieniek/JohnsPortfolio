Get started

Protect a tab in a few clicks.

  1. Install or locally load Sonic Shielding in Chrome or Brave.
  2. Pin the extension for easy access.
  3. Open a tab that plays audio.
  4. Select Protect this tab from the toolbar popup.
  5. Open the comfort profile when you want finer control.

What it protects

Responsive protection, shaped by you.

Beep blocker

Finds narrow electronic tones and briefly softens their frequencies.

Alarm reduction

Recognizes stable multi-tone alarm patterns for stronger optional reduction.

Peak control

Limits sudden whole-sound spikes against your chosen ceiling.

Comfort EQ

Offers nine locally saved frequency bands for persistent personalization.

Know the browser boundary.

Enable protection separately for each tab. Browser security prevents capture on internal browser pages and extension-store pages. Sonic Shielding cannot change operating-system sounds or audio from desktop applications.
